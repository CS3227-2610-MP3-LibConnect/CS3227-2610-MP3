## Key decisions and rationale
- Use `/member/dashboard` for members and `/librarian/dashboard` for librarians (user-confirmed).
- The authenticated session's server-provided `accountType` determines dashboard access. `/` and successful login route by that account type; a saved path to the other role's dashboard is rejected.
- Client route checks control page rendering; existing server-side authorization remains authoritative for business APIs.
- Preserve the no-JavaScript login redirect to `/`; the client resolves `/` after restoring the session.
