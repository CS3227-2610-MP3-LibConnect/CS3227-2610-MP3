# Reflections

This is the reflection for LibConnect. It connects the two individual
reflections:

- [Jun Hong's reflection](reflections/junhong.md) covers authentication, the
  application foundation, and the member track. This includes the member
  dashboard, borrowing, loans, reservations, fines, notifications, profile, and
  the Libby member assistant.
- [Jiali's reflection](reflections/jiali.md) covers the librarian and
  librarian manager track. This includes manager account activation and
  deactivation, librarian password reset, catalog and inventory, loan
  return/loss review, reservations, fines, notifications, member account
  support, and the librarian AI review assistant.
