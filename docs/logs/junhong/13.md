## Key decisions and rationale

- My Reservations pending and history views both use bounded pagination (user-selected).
- Cancelling an assigned reservation releases its copy but preserves reservation `copyId` and `expiryDate` as history (user-selected).
- Member loan closure reports set the copy to `IN_TRANSIT` when accepted (user-selected during the My Loans design).
- Renewal is allowed with pending or assigned reservations; due-date eligibility lasts through the full `Asia/Singapore` calendar day; the 30-day extension is anchored to the existing due timestamp (user-confirmed).
- The project boundary keeps librarian queue, assignment, notification, expiry, and review workflows outside member feature designs.
